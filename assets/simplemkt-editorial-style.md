# SimpleMkt Editorial Art Direction

When using the SimpleMkt default, apply this visual language to the cover and every content image. The cover and content images are one series, but they have different jobs: the cover is a PPT-cover presentation and content images are explanatory diagrams. There is no landscape photography, pale-green outer frame, paper tint, warm color cast, or generic SVG/wireframe treatment.

## Canvas

- Use the fixed canvas dimensions required by the package.
- Use one opaque, pure solid white `#FFFFFF` editorial surface across the full canvas. Material detail belongs only inside explanatory elements and must never tint the canvas.
- Keep all titles, labels, arrows, and notes inside a generous 5% safe area. The top-right Logo reserve is stricter and has priority.

## Shared editorial language

- Use one high-contrast Chinese Songti-like editorial-serif family for every visible Chinese string. On the fixed 1200×675 body-figure canvas, set the core judgment at 52px semibold in exact `#1A6B3A`; set its one-line subtitle at 28px regular in exact `#6B6B6B`; set structural headings at 32px medium and paper-node labels at 28px regular, both in exact `#141414`; set the lower-left disclosure or note at 16px italic in exact `#8C8C8C`. English technical identifiers use restrained upright `Inter Variable` only when a clear Chinese label is unavailable.
- Use this editorial vocabulary for non-text elements: a small excerpt sheet for a source or claim; a straight-edged narrow paper strip for a process stage, judgment, or output; a cut-paper field for a decision, boundary, or contained scope; direct ink marks, hatch, or circular anchors for a position or measured change. Reuse the same primitive for the same role within one figure.
- Make every core element depict its role-specific information. A source/claim sheet exposes relevant lines or a highlight; a relationship exposes selected nodes and links; an explanatory figure exposes a framed or annotated relation; a reader takeaway exposes the resolved reading outcome. Readable fictional microcopy is permitted when it clarifies the explanation and follows the disclosure rule below.
- Derive one grammar-matched editorial engraving or pencil-sketch subject from the article's stated relation or concrete source detail. Use controlled contour lines and restrained cross-hatching or print grain. The subject carries the explanation through direct printed labels, thin leaders, and small circular anchors; it is never detached decoration.
- Keep paper primitives in one family: white, subtly fibrous, straight- or cut-edge, with the same deliberately faint lift (approximately 4–6px-equivalent elevation and one diffuse neutral 12–16px-equivalent shadow at 4–6% opacity). Use thin, low-contrast 1–1.5px-equivalent forest-green or light-gray linework. Construction lines may frame the subject, terminate a leader, or clarify a position inside the core diagram.
- Keep the top-right Logo reserve wholly unoccupied and pure white. Do not render a wordmark or allow title, subtitle, label, connector, or diagram content to enter that reserve.

## Cover presentation

- Treat the cover as a normal PPT cover, not as a content image: compose its approved title at prominent cover scale with one dominant visual metaphor in a calm, continuous title-safe zone. Deliberate title line breaks are allowed only for readability.
- Render no text other than the approved title by default. Subtitles, labels, source excerpts, data, notes, microcopy, and other title-external text require an explicit request.
- The cover does not need a visual-grammar topology, explanatory labels, relation words, short explanation, or evidence note. It must not turn the article into a flowchart or a generic report slide.

## Content-image composition

- Maintain `core judgment → short explanation → diagram → evidence note when needed`, generous whitespace, and one dominant relationship. Preserve the selected visual grammar and source topology.
- The planned `visible_title` is the content image's centered core judgment, not a small corner heading. On the 1200×675 canvas, fix the centered title/subtitle band to `x=600`, `y=76–176`: place the 52px semibold title and its 28px subtitle inside that band, leave a 16px gap below the Logo reserve ending at `y=60`, and begin the diagram at or below `y=200`.
- Use exactly one core structure under that judgment. A linked reading chain such as `source paragraph or claim → selected change → explanatory graphic → user conclusion` is welcome when it is the chosen grammar. Detail is welcome when every component serves that one reading path and user takeaway.
- When the plan requires an illustrative disclosure, render it in the lower-left safe area using the fixed 16px italic `#8C8C8C` note style. It is a disclosure, not a centered caption or diagram component.
